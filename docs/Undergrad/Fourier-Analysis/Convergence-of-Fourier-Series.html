Theorem: Suppose f and g are two integrable functions defined on the circle, and for some θ0​, there exists an open interval I containing θ0​ such that f(θ)=g(θ) for all θ∈I, then SN​(f)(θ0​)−SN​(g)(θ0​)→0 as N tends to infinity

This happens because f−g is 0 in I and therefore differentiable at θ0​, so we can apply our convergence theorem
